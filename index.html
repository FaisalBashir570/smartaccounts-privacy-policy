SmartAccounts Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

SmartAccounts is an offline-first business accounting and ledger application designed for merchants, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, and field sales teams.

1. Information Stored in the App

SmartAccounts allows users to store business information on their device, including customer names, shop names, phone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, addresses, cities, areas, ledger records, debit and credit transactions, balances, and local user account information.

2. Local Storage

The information entered into SmartAccounts is stored locally on the user's device. SmartAccounts does not require a remote account server for normal operation.

3. Data Transmission

SmartAccounts does not transmit customers' business records, ledger transactions, or locally stored user information to SmartAccounts developer servers or third-party analytics services.

4. Advertising and Tracking

SmartAccounts does not contain advertising services, analytics tracking, or third-party advertising SDKs.

5. Backups

SmartAccounts provides user-controlled manual backup and restore functionality. Backup files are protected using password-based encryption. Users may choose to save or manually share a backup file using the Android sharing system.

Users are responsible for protecting any backup files they save or share. SmartAccounts does not automatically upload these backup files to a developer server.

6. Passwords and Security

User passwords are protected using secure password hashing techniques. Security-sensitive information is not intentionally stored in plaintext within backup files.

7. User Accounts

SmartAccounts can support local user roles such as Administrator, Salesman, and Sales Officer. These local accounts are used for access control and business data management on the device.

8. Account and Data Deletion

An authorized Administrator can delete staff user accounts through the User Management section of the application.

Because SmartAccounts stores its user accounts locally on the device and does not maintain a remote user database, deletion of a local user account is performed on the device.

9. Third-Party Services

SmartAccounts does not intentionally share customer, ledger, or user account information with third-party advertising or analytics services.

10. Children's Privacy

SmartAccounts is designed for business and professional use and is not directed toward children.

11. Data Retention

Data remains on the user's device until the user or an authorized Administrator deletes it, resets the application data, or removes the application. Manually exported backup files may remain wherever the user chooses to save them until the user deletes them.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SmartAccounts functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be made available on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or support regarding SmartAccounts, please use the official support contact provided in the SmartAccounts Google Play Store listing.
